The meaning

The researcher’s twin. Says what the evidence adds up to, then has to defend it.

The challenge

the skeptic

A model from a different AI company tries to break every claim.

The call

the referee

When the Skeptic won’t let go, a model from yet another AI company makes the call.

The brief

the editor

Writes the brief from the claims that held.

The wording

the reviewer

Checks the brief’s wording against the claims, so it says no more than the evidence does.

The checks

the code

Checks every passage, number and sentence before a brief is approved.
